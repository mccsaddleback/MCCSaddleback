Coconut Tofu Noodle Soup

October 22, 2026

Serves 2 · About 20 minutes

Coconut milk makes a regular packet of instant noodles feel like a whole different meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 packets instant noodles
  • ½ package tofu, drained and cubed
  • 1 cup canned coconut milk, stirred before measuring
  • 2 cups water
  • ½ onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Instant noodle seasoning, to taste
  • Optional: curry powder, chili sauce, or a squeeze of lime

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan. Cook the onion over medium heat for about 5 minutes.
  2. If using curry powder, stir in 1 teaspoon and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the coconut milk, water, and tofu. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles and cook according to their package directions.
  5. Stir in a little noodle seasoning at a time, tasting before adding more. Finish with any optional toppings.
