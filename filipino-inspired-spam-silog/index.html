Filipino-Inspired SPAM Silog

October 22, 2026

Cuisine: Filipino-Inspired
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 2
Difficulty: Easy

Description

Silog is a popular Filipino breakfast featuring rice, a fried egg, and a savory main ingredient. This version uses crispy SPAM for a simple, satisfying meal that works any time of day!

Ingredients

  • ½ can SPAM, sliced

  • 2 eggs

  • 2 cups cooked white rice (or cooked lentil-and-rice mixture)

  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)

  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

  • Vinegar or hot sauce, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the rice. Cook rice according to the package instructions.

  2. Make garlic rice. If garlic is available, heat a little oil in a skillet and sauté the minced garlic until fragrant. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.

  3. Fry the SPAM. Cut SPAM into slices and cook in a skillet over medium heat until browned on both sides.

  4. Cook the eggs. Fry the eggs to your preferred doneness, making sure they are cooked thoroughly.

  5. Assemble the plate. Serve the rice alongside the SPAM and fried eggs.

  6. Enjoy! Add vinegar or hot sauce for extra flavor if desired.

Cultural Note

The word silog comes from sinangag (garlic fried rice) and itlog (egg). Filipino silog breakfasts have many variations using different meats and proteins. This recipe is a pantry-friendly adaptation!
