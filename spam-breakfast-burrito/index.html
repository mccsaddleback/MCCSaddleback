SPAM Breakfast Burrito

October 22, 2026

 

Cuisine: Mexican-American / Hawaiian-Inspired
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 2
Difficulty: Easy

Description

Looking for a quick and filling breakfast? This SPAM Breakfast Burrito combines crispy SPAM, fluffy scrambled eggs, golden potatoes, and sautéed vegetables, all wrapped in a warm tortilla. It's an easy way to turn everyday pantry ingredients into a delicious meal!

Ingredients

  • ½ can SPAM, diced

  • 2 large flour tortillas

  • 2 eggs

  • 1 medium potato, diced

  • ½ onion, chopped

  • 1 jalapeño, chopped (optional)

  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

  • Black pepper, to taste

  • Hot sauce (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the potatoes. Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add diced potatoes and cook for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender and golden.

  2. Add the vegetables. Stir in chopped onion and jalapeño. Cook for 3–4 minutes until softened.

  3. Crisp the SPAM. Add diced SPAM and cook until lightly browned, about 4–5 minutes.

  4. Scramble the eggs. Push the other ingredients to one side of the pan. Add the beaten eggs and gently scramble until fully cooked.

  5. Assemble the burritos. Warm the tortillas, divide the filling evenly between them, and wrap tightly.

  6. Serve and enjoy! Add hot sauce or your favorite toppings if available.

Pantry Tip

SPAM is already salty, so you probably won't need extra salt. For an even more filling burrito, add canned chickpeas or lentils.
