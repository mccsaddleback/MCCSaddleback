Tomato & White Bean Pasta

October 22, 2026

Serves 3–4 · About 25 minutes

A simple pantry version of pasta e fagioli: pasta and beans cooked in a thick tomato broth.

Ingredients

  • 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1½ cups dry mini penne
  • ½ onion, diced
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 4 cups water
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: garlic, Italian seasoning, or chili flakes

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and any optional seasonings. Cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the water and beans. Bring to a boil, then stir in the pasta.
  4. Simmer, stirring frequently, until the pasta is tender, about 10–15 minutes. Add more water if it gets too thick.
  5. Mash a few beans against the side of the pot to thicken the broth. Season to taste.
