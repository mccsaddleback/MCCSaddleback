Multicultural Center Recipies

Not sure what to make with the ingredients you picked up from the food pantry? The Multicultural Center cookbook is a place to find recipes from different cultures and try something new. We’re putting together simple, affordable meals that use pantry items, along with a little background on the dishes and the people who share them. Whether you cook all the time or are just figuring it out, we hope you find something you’re excited to make!
